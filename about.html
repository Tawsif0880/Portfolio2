Background

I am building a career around teaching, clarity, and long-term academic contribution.

I currently serve as a Teaching Assistant at North South University, where I support students by breaking down difficult concepts into manageable explanations. My academic background in mathematics and physics has strengthened both my reasoning and my ability to approach problems methodically.

Teaching is the work that feels most meaningful to me. I value the moment when a student moves from confusion to confidence, and that is the direction shaping my long-term goal of becoming a university faculty member.

What I bring Logical reasoning, patience, quick adaptation, and a consistent focus on student understanding.
How I work Structured explanations, dependable preparation, and a calm approach to complex material.
